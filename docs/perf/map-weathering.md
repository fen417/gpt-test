# Map material pass
Independent map weathering off / low / high. One procedural 256x256 RGBA mipmapped mask, no external downloads or new draw calls. Low samples the coarse mask once; high adds a surface-oriented sample for grime, dust and sparse cracks, faded at distance. Shader-based decals, not hand-placed objects. World coordinates break repetition independently of mesh UVs. New PBR textures receive modest material-specific tile scale corrections; existing UVs, concrete/rock POM coordinates and original assets remain intact. Water, glass, signs and authored fabric/paint atlases are preserved.

Profiles: dusty Village and campaign desert maps, mineral/soil variation in Quarry, darker industrial grime in Port/Nahar, restrained wear in Laguna/Linden Estate. Quarry cliffs, desert mountains, fallback village ground and plaster now opt into PBR. Both render APIs have separate shader implementations. Legacy presets keep weathering off; Ultra enables it. Adaptive quality can reduce high to low, or turn it off with minimum policy. No gameplay/collision changes. Production build and unit tests do not replace visual verification on actual WebGL/WebGPU hardware.
