# Menu music

`quiet-resolve.m4a` is the complete AAC stereo runtime copy of `Quiet Resolve.mp3` supplied by the project owner from Downloads. The unchanged original is preserved at `art-source/audio/music/quiet-resolve.mp3`. Track title: **Quiet Resolve**. No artist metadata has been inferred.

The menu streams and loops the local asset through the music volume bus. Playback pauses when entering a match or muting music and resumes when returning to the menu.
